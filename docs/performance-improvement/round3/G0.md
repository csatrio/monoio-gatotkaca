# Gate G0: kernel statements, D1 reproduced, D2 diagnosed, baselines

Tree: `r3-wave0` (wave 0 merged: r3-stats, r3-repro, r3-probe). Machine quiet, `performance` governor, Linux
7.0.0-42. Numbers from the fork's `reqresp` and `smallframes` examples are screening (server on CPU 2, clients on
3 and 4, one process). The one bench run is in `../gatotkaca-transport/docs/run/run122.md`.

## 1. Kernel statements

Evidence (source quotes at v7.0, probe output) is in [r3-probe.md](r3-probe.md).

| id | verdict | what later packages must use |
|---|---|---|
| KV1 | partly true | One run of deferred task work handles at most max(20, `min_complete`) items and sets `IORING_SQ_TASKRUN` again when items are left. The cap exists since **6.13**. An enter with `GETEVENTS` that also submits gets two runs (41 items with `min_complete` ≤ 1). An enter that **slept** delivers exactly `min_complete` items on wake-up (one for `want = 1`). An enter without `GETEVENTS` runs none. The flag is reliable for "work is left". |
| KV2 | true | A failed link member with `CQE_SKIP_SUCCESS` posts its CQE; the members after it post nothing. SEND(`IO_LINK \| SKIP`) → RECV posts **exactly one CQE in every case**. A tag bit in `user_data` is needed to tell which member posted it. ROUND3.md C1's "the receive's CQE always arrives" is wrong for a head with the flag. |
| KV3 | true, with additions | `MSG_WAITALL` is required (without it a short send is skipped as a success). A send that fails after partial progress reports the **byte count**, not an errno. After 127 poll re-arms the send moves to io-wq and the linked `RECV(POLL_FIRST)` then posts `-EAGAIN` ("send complete, receive not started"). |
| KV4 | true without SQPOLL | The kernel copies `msghdr` and iovec array when it prepares the SQE (inside `io_uring_enter`; on SQPOLL rings in the SQ thread, later). Rule for A1: keep both until the op's CQE. An inline `msghdr` in a movable future is unsound. |
| KV5 | true | `IORING_SEND_VECTORIZED` = `1 << 5` in `sqe->ioprio`, since 6.17, works on 7.0 for plain SEND (also with `MSG_WAITALL`, as a link head, and for `SEND_ZC`). No feature bit: older kernels answer `-EINVAL` at prep. |

Consequences:
- **D1**: the drain is `enter(0, GETEVENTS)` while the flag is set; each delivers at most 20 completions. A drain
  with `want = cq.len()` raises the cap but is only valid before `tick()` reaps the CQ. A drain must not go
  through the `want > 0` branch of `UringInner::enter` (it adds `min_wait`).
- **C1**: one slot, one CQE, tag bit; `MSG_WAITALL` on the send; handle `-EAGAIN` on the receive and a
  non-negative count on a failed send; cancel both `user_data` values on drop; no `SEND_ZC` head. On a `defer`
  ring the linked receive is started by a deferred task-work item, which counts against the cap of 20.
- **A1**: the per-thread `msghdr` free list (E8) stands; vectored SEND is a real option on 6.17+.

## 2. D1 reproduced locally (in direction, smaller than on the bench)

`reqresp --conns 9 --bulk 2 --path pipe --bulk-work W --ring <ring>,skipenter,ringfd`, `metrics` build, 4 s windows.
`--bulk-work` (added at this gate) does W bytes of byte-serial user-space work on each bulk chunk, a stand-in for
the bench server's stream encryption.

| W | ring | repl frames/s | bulk MB/s | frames per round | RECV same tick | waiting enters that left task work |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | coop | 74,497 | 560 | 8.73 | 97.3% | 2.6% |
| 0 | defer | 72,209 | 600 | 7.88 | 87.9% | 45.3% |
| 4k | coop | 66,211 | 501 | 8.66 | 96.8% | 2.3% |
| 4k | defer | 63,519 | 520 | 8.01 | 88.3% | 47.5% |
| 8k | coop | 57,286 | 450 | 8.34 | 94.3% | 2.9% |
| 8k | defer | 55,939 | 461 | 7.95 | 87.5% | 47.4% |
| 16k | coop | 45,834 | 343 | 8.76 | 98.1% | 1.1% |
| 16k | defer | 43,958 | 366 | 7.88 | 86.8% | 47.8% |

- `defer` leaves task work behind after about 47% of its waiting enters (`enter_left_taskrun / enter_wait`),
  `coop` after 1–3%. `defer` serves 3–4% fewer frames and 4–7% more bulk.
- The bench's split is larger (96% against 52% same tick, −16% frames). The reproducer's client answers faster
  than `wrk`. The mechanism and the direction are the same, so the local check for r3-drain is: with
  `drain<N>` the left-behind share and the same-tick share reach the `coop` values.
- KV1 explains the mechanism: the park enter gets two runs of task work, then returns because the bulk sends'
  CQEs exist. Frames that arrive after the second run stay queued until the next enter.

## 3. D2 diagnosed: one accept per round, not the driver

**Mechanism.** On a `COOP_TASKRUN` ring one enter runs all pending task work, so with 2000 closed-loop
connections every connection completes in the same tick and the loop falls into one round per sweep:

| `reqresp --conns 2000 --sizes 4k --path pipe` | frames/s | rounds/s | frames per round | p99 | longest gap of any connection |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `coop,skipenter,ringfd,fixedfd` | 98,776 | 49 | 2000 | 21.2 ms | 22 ms |
| `defer,skipenter,ringfd,fixedfd` | 109,039 | 2,725 | 40 | 22.8 ms | 29 ms |

- No connection is starved in the driver: the longest gap is one round. The two candidates of the plan are ruled
  out: SQ pressure (`--entries` 1024, 2048, 16384 and 2100 connections: p99 23–27 ms with and without
  `sq_full_flushes`) and late CQEs (`recv_same_tick` is 100% under `coop`).
- The reproducer accepts with `accept_batch_into`. The bench server's default loop awaits one `accept_with` per
  scheduler round (round 2, M1). At 50 rounds a second the 2000 connections `wrk` opens at the start of the
  measured window wait in the accept queue for seconds.

**Proof on the bench** (run 122, R2000, 1 round, same binary, only the accept loop differs):

| variant | frames/s | ack p50 | ack p90 | ack p99 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `coop:skipenter:pipeack:sweep:ringfd:fixedfd` | 96,101 | 16.2 ms | 1.17 s | 7.11 s | 9.99 s |
| the same with `acceptmulti` | 95,606 | 20.6 ms | 21.1 ms | 22.2 ms | 44.7 ms |

- With multishot accept the ack p99 is 22.2 ms, against 19.4 ms for `defer` (ROUND3.md): inside the 2× bound of
  the D2 acceptance.
- **Outcome**: no driver fix. The rule goes into AGENTS.md (r3-docs): on a `coop_taskrun` ring with many
  connections the loop makes one round per sweep of all connections, so anything that does one operation per
  round is throttled to that rate; accept with `accept_batch_into` (or `accept_multi`). The bench should make
  that its default accept loop.
- What remains of D2 is the throughput: the one-round-per-sweep regime is about 10% slower than `defer` here
  (98,776 against 109,039). That is `COOP_TASKRUN`'s own behaviour; "many connections → `defer`" stays the rule.

## 4. Baselines (tree `r3-wave0`, default build, medians of 3 runs of 5 s)

`reqresp --ring defer,skipenter,ringfd`:

| connections | path | deadline | frames/s | p99 | user instr / frame | user cycles / frame | all cycles / frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 (4/16/64 KiB) | plain | none | 93,853 | 158 µs | 2,622 | 1,847 | 45,596 |
| 8 | plain | timeout | 92,605 | 166 µs | 3,528 | 2,430 | 46,263 |
| 8 | pipe | none | 92,247 | 178 µs | 2,077 | 1,534 | 46,439 |
| 8 | pipe | timeout | 92,096 | 178 µs | 3,033 | 1,959 | 46,530 |
| 2000 (4 KiB) | plain | none | 108,782 | 19.1 ms | 2,398 | 2,521 | 39,400 |
| 2000 | plain | timeout | 103,500 | 20.2 ms | 3,310 | 3,662 | 41,381 |
| 2000 | pipe | none | 108,533 | 19.1 ms | 2,342 | 2,363 | 39,464 |
| 2000 | pipe | timeout | 107,601 | 19.1 ms | 3,341 | 3,130 | 39,810 |

`smallframes --conns 2000 --size 4096 --client-cpus 3,4`: 2,671 user instructions per frame with the per-frame
`timeout` (117,845 / 115,824 / 113,774 frames/s), 1,884 without.

- A `time::timeout` per frame costs 790–1,000 user instructions per frame, 30–40% of the server's user-space
  work. T1 is the largest single item for the O1 target.
- User space is 4–6% of all cycles per frame. The rest is the kernel, as ROUND3.md says.

## Changes to the plan

- KV1: "6.12+" → "6.13+", plus the wake-up cap.
- r3-drain: no D2 fix. It builds the drain after the first `tick()` and, as a second variant for the lead to
  compare, a drain before `tick()` with `want = cq.len()`.
- D2 is closed by the bench run above; G1 only re-checks it with the bench's accept loop switched.
- r3-pair and r3-vec get the consequences listed in section 1.
