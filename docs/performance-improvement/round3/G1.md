# Gate G1: wave 1 on the bench

Tree: `r3-wave1` plus the removal of the pre-tick drain variant (`c33cad3`). Bench: `../gatotkaca-transport`
working tree with the new `--tune` items (`drain<N>`, `eagerack`, `deadline`, `wset`, `wset72`, `direct`) and
`[patch.crates-io]` on this tree; results in `results/r3-g1-*`, run docs 123–131. Machine quiet, `performance`
governor, 3 rounds per cell (R8 and R2000: 10 s; MIX: 15 s). Verdicts by the bound ROUND3.md gives for the item,
else the 8% rule.

Every monoio variant carries `acceptmulti` (G0: the single-accept loop is what broke R2000 under `coop`); the
`direct` variant uses batch direct accept instead. Variant names below drop the common part:
`FEW` = `coop:skipenter:pipeack:sweep:ringfd:fixedfd:acceptmulti`,
`MANY` = `defer:skipenter:pipeack:mshot:ringfd:fixedfd:acceptmulti`,
`D` = `defer:skipenter:pipeack:sweep:ringfd:fixedfd:acceptmulti`.

## Quiet-slot confirmations (reproducer, medians of 3)

- **Drain variants.** Mixed load (9 conns + 2 bulk, `--bulk-work 8k`): `defer` 57,983 frames/s, `drain1` 58,987,
  `drain4` 59,351, pre-tick `draincq4` 59,250, `coop` 59,724; same-tick share 92% → 96–98% (coop 99%). 2000
  connections: `defer` 112,511, `drain1` 111,449 (−0.9%), `drain4` 109,586 (−2.6%), `draincq4` 97,615 (−13%,
  every round carries all 2000 frames). **Variant B removed** (`c33cad3`); variant A kept.
- **E5 (64-byte slot)** was kept on the agent's exact numbers (−1% instructions on top of its other wins, −10% L1D
  misses, inline storage unchanged at 48 bytes); not re-measured separately.
- **E6 (`write_all`)** kept: the one load where it lost 0.6% (`smallframes` with timeout) gained 4% overall from
  the merge fixups.
- **Merge fixups** (`#[inline(always)]` on the four send/receive constructors, one vtable address for task wakers):
  user instructions per frame, merged tree before → after: pipe 1,843 → 1,798; plain 1,847 → 1,780; eager 1,221 →
  1,201; `Deadline` 2,068 → 1,983; `timeout` 2,505 → 2,433; 8-connection pipe 1,596 → 1,548; `smallframes` 2,141 →
  2,116.

- **D2 passes**: `coop` with multishot accept has ack p99 22.0 ms, within 2× of `defer`'s 18.3 ms (ROUND3.md:
  1.32 s). It stays 13–18% below `defer` in throughput: the one-round-per-sweep regime of `coop` with many
  connections (G0). Rule for AGENTS.md: many connections → `defer_taskrun`, and accept in batches.
- **D1 at R2000**: `drain2` is 1.8–2.1% below the three `MANY` controls, at the 2% bound; p99 ranges overlap.
  `drain1` −3.4% looks like a bad draw (it is below `drain2` on every metric, against the reproducer's order).
  Verdict: at the bound; `drain2` is the value to recommend for a single configuration, `drain4` is not.
- **S1 at R2000 passes**: −1.2%.
- **T1 passes**: `deadline` against the sweeper +0.2% (bound 1%), same latency.
- **B1 at R2000 passes**: `wset` +3.5%, `wset72` +2.1% against `MANY`'s two-ring hot/spill arrangement. The
  ring stays at about 80 published buffers of 4096 after the ramp (reproducer, `metrics`).
- **K1 passes**: direct accept +0.4% against `fixedfd`, with 3 fewer syscalls per connection (0 against 3 from
  accept to close, strace in r3-direct.md).

- **D1 passes with the eager ack**: `drain2:eagerack` is +1.9% against `FEW` (bound: within 3%) and its ack p99
  (197 µs) is below `FEW`'s range (218–226) and below tokio's. The drain alone (`drain2`, `drain4`) brings the
  frames within 1–2% of `FEW` without `minwait` (ROUND3.md: −17% without it), but keeps the batched loop's ack p99
  (293 µs): that part was S1's problem, not D1's. The "same tick ≥ 90%" check was done on the reproducer (97.6%
  with `drain4`, 92% without), not on the bench.
- `drain4` and `drain2` are equal here (+1.2%); `drain2` costs less at R2000.
- The all-multishot form gives up 3% of frames and 30 µs of p99 next to bulk streams; the `pipeack` split (first
  64 connections on per-connection buffers) stays the better shape for few connections.

at 8 connections inside tokio's range instead of +27%.
